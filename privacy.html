NookBird

Privacy statement

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short

  • NookBird runs on a computer in your home. Your family's calendar, reminders, messages and anything it learns are stored there, not on our servers.
  • We never receive your family's information. We only hold what we need to sell you a licence and help you when you ask.
  • Parents and carers decide who can use NookBird, can see what it has learned, and can correct, delete or export anything at any time.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we are

NookBird is operated by Hajar Shalaby (ABN 94 367 193 594), Victoria, Australia. In this statement, "we" and "us" means NookBird. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Your family's information stays in your home

NookBird is software you install on your own Windows computer. Everything it keeps is stored on that computer, including:

  • the family members you add, and their roles (parent or carer, child, or other adult)
  • your calendar, reminders and important dates
  • messages family members send to NookBird
  • emails and school or club calendars you choose to connect, and what NookBird learns from them
  • health notes, only if a parent or carer turns them on (they are then visible to parents and carers only)

We have no server that collects this information, and we can't see it. It stays on your computer until you delete it. A parent or carer can ask NookBird to forget something, which deletes it completely, and can export all of the family's information at any time.

Services your family connects to

To work, NookBird uses accounts that your family sets up and controls. Information goes directly between your computer and these services, never through us:

Telegram and Anthropic may process information outside Australia. Each has its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of their services.

Children

NookBird is made for families, and parents and carers decide who in the family uses it. There is no public sign-up. Family members chat with NookBird in Telegram, so Telegram's own terms, including its age rules, apply to their Telegram accounts.

If a parent or carer does add a child, these protections apply:

  • Only people a parent or carer invites can use NookBird.
  • NookBird is an AI assistant, not a person, and it only helps with family matters.
  • Children see only what is about them or the whole family, never health information or anything kept for parents and carers.
  • Parents and carers can see what NookBird has learned about any family member, and correct or delete it.

If you believe a child is using NookBird without a parent's or carer's knowledge, or you have any concern about a child's safety, please email us at hello@nookbird.com.

What we collect ourselves

When you buy NookBird

Your name, email address and order details, collected by our payment provider when you buy a licence. The payment provider handles your card details; we never see them.

When you activate a licence

You email us a short activation request. It contains your activation code, a code identifying your computer (made from its hardware, not your files), the NookBird version, and your family bot's Telegram ID number. It never contains your family's information, your messages or your bot's access token. We use it only to issue and check your licence.

When you contact us

Whatever you include in your email. If you need help, we may ask you to send a diagnostic file. You choose whether to send it, and we use it only to fix your problem.

Updates

NookBird checks a public download page on GitHub for updates. No family information is sent; as with any download, GitHub can see your computer's internet address.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' internet addresses for security.

How we use and keep it

We use the information we collect only to sell and license NookBird, provide updates, answer your questions and meet our legal obligations. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising. We keep purchase and licence records for as long as you hold a licence and as tax law requires, and other emails only as long as we need them.

We store our records securely and limit who can see them.

Access, correction and complaints

Your family's information is on your own computer, so you can see, correct, delete or export it directly in NookBird. To see or correct the information we hold about you, or to make a complaint about how we handled it, email hello@nookbird.com. We will reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
