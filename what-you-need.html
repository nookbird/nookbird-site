What you need for NookBird

NookBird is a private app. It runs on your own computer at home, never on our servers, so your family's information stays with you. That's why it needs a few things from your home.

A Windows 11 computer, ideally left on

NookBird works quietly in the background. We recommend leaving the computer on and connected to the internet, so your reminders and briefs arrive on time. It doesn't have to be on all the time: if you restart the computer, or the power goes out, NookBird starts again by itself when the computer is back on. A laptop at home is fine.

Telegram on each family member's phone

Telegram is a free messaging app. NookBird talks to your family through your own private Telegram bot, which you make during setup with step-by-step pictures.

A Gmail address for NookBird

NookBird currently works with Gmail. We strongly recommend making a new Gmail address just for NookBird to read, and never giving it access to your personal or work email. You then set your own email to forward the school, kinder and club emails to the new address. Our guide shows you how.

Only Windows 11 for now

NookBird supports Windows 11 computers only at the moment; we plan to add more. If your home doesn't have one, ask us to tell you when NookBird is ready, and tell us what you use.

NookBird is made in Australia, for Australian families.
