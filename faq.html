Frequently asked questions

Who is NookBird for?

Australian families with children at school or kinder. Parents and carers set it up and decide who in the family uses it.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer at home, ideally left on, and Telegram on your family's phones. Our guide walks you through setup, and we help by email if you get stuck. More on what you need.

How much does it cost?

We'll announce our prices before NookBird is ready for every family, and if you've asked us to notify you, you'll hear first.

Is NookBird a person or an AI?

NookBird is an AI assistant, not a person. It only helps with family matters.

Does NookBird read my personal email?

NookBird can only read the emails in the email address you choose to connect. We strongly recommend setting up a separate Gmail address just for NookBird, and never connecting your personal or work email.

Who can see my family's information?

Your family's information stays on your own computer, not on our servers, and we can't see it. To do its job, NookBird uses the services you connect: your Gmail address, Telegram for your family's messages, and Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to read the emails. Anthropic doesn't use your family's information to train its AI. Our privacy statement sets out exactly what goes where.

Can children use NookBird?

Parents and carers decide who in the family uses it. A child sees only what is about them or the whole family. Family members chat with NookBird in Telegram, so Telegram's own terms, including its age rules, apply.

What if the computer is turned off?

NookBird works while your computer is on. If it's off, some briefs and reminders may arrive late or be missed, so it's best to leave it on.

Why a few families at a time?

So every new family gets a proper setup and someone to ask. We'd rather grow a little slower and have every family glad they joined.

How do I stop using NookBird?

Whenever you like. You can export everything, ask it to forget anything, or remove it from your computer, without asking us.

Didn't find your answer?

Email us at hello@nookbird.com. If you're using NookBird and need help, see Support.

NookBird is made in Australia, for Australian families.
