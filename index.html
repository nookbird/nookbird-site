Questions

Who is NookBird for?

Australian families with children at school or kinder. Parents and carers set it up and decide who in the family uses it.

What do I need?

A Windows 11 computer at home that can stay on, and Telegram on your family's phones. We help you set everything up.

How private is it?

NookBird runs in your home, and your family's information is never sent to us. Our privacy statement sets out exactly what goes where.

How much does it cost?

We'll announce prices in January 2027, before Term 1. Families on the waitlist hear first.

Why by invitation?

So every new family gets a proper setup and someone to ask. We'd rather grow a little slower and have every family glad they joined.
