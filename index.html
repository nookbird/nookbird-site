The family assistant that keeps everyone on the same page.

NookBird is a private AI assistant for your household. Your family talks to it in Telegram, and it helps you keep track of who needs to be where and what's due.

Coming to Australian families in early 2027

What NookBird does

A shared family calendar

Add, change and check events just by asking.

Reminders for each person

Hats on Thursday, permission slips on Friday, sent to the right person.

School and club emails, sorted

Forward them in, and NookBird picks out the dates and notices.

A short daily brief

A morning and evening summary for parents.

Private by design

NookBird runs on a computer in your home. Your family's information stays there and is never sent to us.

Built for families, with children in mind

  • Parents set up NookBird and decide who can use it. There is no public sign-up.
  • NookBird is an AI assistant, not a person, and it only talks about family matters.
  • Children see only what is about them, never health or parents-only information.
  • Your family's information stays on your own computer and is never sent to us.
  • Parents can see what NookBird has learned and correct or delete anything.
