Know what matters for your kids. Skip everything that doesn't.

Newsletters, club updates, payment requests, excursion notes, last‑minute changes. They pour in every week, and the one email that really matters is easy to miss among the dozens that don't.

NookBird reads them for you. It picks out what matters to your family (the dates, the payments, the forms, and who each one is for) and leaves the rest out of your way. The parents get one short brief each morning and evening, everyone gets their own reminders through Telegram, and the family calendar keeps itself up to date.

  • No more digging through a newsletter for the one date you need.
  • Forms, payments, excursions and early finishes, pointed out to you in time.
  • No more being the family's reminder service. NookBird tells the right person at the right time.

A small group of families from any state will use it for real during Term 1, 2027 and tell us what works. NookBird runs on your own Windows 11 computer at home. Here is the whole deal.

On your own Windows 11 computer

NookBird installs on a Windows 11 PC at home and works quietly in the background. We recommend leaving the computer on, so reminders and briefs arrive on time; if you restart it, NookBird starts again by itself. A laptop at home is fine. You install it yourself, with our installer and a one-page guide. If you get stuck, email support@nookbird.com.

What NookBird does

  • A short brief for the parents, morning and evening. What's on, what needs doing and what's new, in two minutes.
  • Reminders to the right person. A child's reminder goes to that child; a payment reminder goes to the parents.
  • Talk to it in plain words. Tell it something has changed or isn't yours, and it sorts it out.
  • Answers from the mail you forward. "When is camp?" or "how much is the incursion?": ask, and it answers from the email.
  • It gets to know your family. The schools, classes and clubs that matter to you, with nothing to set up.

You choose what NookBird sees

NookBird needs its own Gmail account, a new address made just for it. It never reads your personal inbox.

From your own email, you set up automatic forwarding to that address for the emails you want NookBird to know about and act on: the school's, the clubs', or any other sender you choose. Our step‑by‑step guide shows you how.

NookBird sees only what you forward, so you are always in control of what it can see. That is also the most we could ever see, and only if you choose to share to help us improve NookBird. Stop forwarding a sender at any time, and NookBird stops hearing from them.

  1. Type your email below and press Apply. We email you seven quick questions; just reply. About five minutes.

The tester agreement

Read the agreement (version 1.6, 17 November 2026)

Between NookBird (www.nookbird.com; "we") and your family ("you"), for testing NookBird in Term 1, 2027. Plain words on purpose.

1. What testing is

NookBird is a family assistant that runs in your home, on your own Windows 11 computer, and talks to your family through your own Telegram bot. It reads the school and club mail that arrives in a mailbox made for it, keeps the family calendar and reminders, and sends the parents a short brief morning and evening. You are testing an early version so that we can make it good before it goes on sale. It will have faults; finding them is the point.

2. What we provide

  • NookBird for your own computer: you install it yourself with our installer and guide, with our help by email if you get stuck. Updated through the term with weekly fixes.
  • The AI it uses, on an account we hold and pay for (section 5).
  • Help: a /feedback command in the bot, a short check-in from us now and then, and email to support@nookbird.com.
  • A free place costs nothing. A paid place costs the reduced monthly price we tell you before you accept it, and can be cancelled at any time.

3. What you provide

  • A Windows 11 PC at home, ideally left on, with an administrator account for the install.
  • A new Gmail account you own, just for NookBird, made before you install, with our guide, and automatic forwarding to it from your own email for the emails you choose. NookBird reads only what you forward. Your family's Telegram bot is made during setup, on your phone; it is yours.
  • Your intent to use it. Places are limited, so by joining you say that you mean to use NookBird through the term as part of normal life and to tell us at the end what you thought. Along the way, honest feedback: a one‑line /feedback is enough; nothing formal.
  • Care with the secrets the setup gives you (the bot token, the mailbox password): they stay in NookBird and are not shared.

4. Your family's information

  • It stays in your home. NookBird keeps your mail, records and chat in its own folder on your computer. It does not send them to us unless you choose to share them (section 6).
  • Children. A child may have their own access to the bot; they see only their own things and never a parent's. Parents see everything.
  • You can always get it out or get rid of it. Tell the bot to forget anything, or clear all of it. Export everything as a zip or uninstall at any time, without our help.
  • What leaves your home: the text NookBird sends to the AI to read or write (section 5); a once‑a‑day check for updates that sends only the version number; the one activation request (a code and a fingerprint of the machine, nothing about the family); the weekly check-up; and, only when you say yes, what you show us about a problem (section 6). Nothing else.

5. The AI

NookBird uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to read mail and write briefs and answers. It runs on an account we hold and pay for, so you need not set one up. That means the text it processes passes through our Anthropic account: we can see how much is used (the cost), never what was said. Anthropic's terms for business use apply (your content is not used to train their models).

6. Feedback, the weekly numbers and what we may use

Once a week, NookBird sends us a short check-up. It never includes your messages, your emails or anything in them. We use it, your ratings and your feedback to improve the product.

Showing us more is your choice, each time. If something goes wrong, we may ask to see how NookBird handled it, including the emails and messages involved. You say yes or no each time. We use what we see only to find and fix that fault, never share it, and delete any copy when the fault is fixed. Saying no changes nothing about your place.

If we ever want to quote something you told us, we ask first.

7. What we do not promise

This is a test of early software. It may miss something, get a date wrong, or stop for a while. Do not rely on it alone for anything that matters; the school's own mail is still the source of truth. We are not responsible for a missed deadline, a wrong reminder or a lost message during testing, and we will fix what you tell us about as fast as we can. Nothing in this agreement limits rights you have under Australian Consumer Law.

8. Leaving

You can stop at any time, for any reason, by telling us; we help you export, and help you uninstall NookBird. We may end testing or your part in it with a week's notice, with the same help; if you have a paid place, we stop charging you. Testing ends at the end of Term 1, 2027. Active testers will then be offered a discount on their first year, with no obligation.
