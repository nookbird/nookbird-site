What NookBird does

  • A brief, morning and evening. 6:30 am: what's on today, what needs doing, what's new. 7:30 pm: tomorrow, open actions, the week ahead. Two minutes to read, and nothing slips.
  • Reminders to the right person. "Bring your hat Thursday" goes to the child it concerns; the payment reminder goes to the parents. Done or Later, one tap.
  • Answers from your own mail. "When is camp?", "how much is the incursion?", "what did the teacher say about the excursion?" Ask in plain words; it has read the email.
  • A shared family calendar that fills itself from the school's mail, with the club's dates beside it.

The pilot agreement

Read the agreement (version 1.1, 12 October 2026)

Between NookBird (www.nookbird.com; "we") and your family ("you"), for the Term 4 2026 pilot. Plain words on purpose.

1. What the pilot is

NookBird is a family assistant that runs on your own PC and talks to your family through your own Telegram bot. It reads the school and club mail that arrives in a mailbox made for it, keeps the family calendar and reminders, and sends the parents a short brief morning and evening. You are testing an early version so that we can make it good before it goes on sale. It will have faults; finding them is the point.

2. What we provide

  • NookBird installed on your PC, set up with you, and updated through the term with weekly fixes.
  • The AI it uses, on an account we hold and pay for, until 31 December 2026 (section 5).
  • Help: a /feedback command in the bot, a short chat with us every two weeks, and a direct line to us for anything urgent.
  • No charge of any kind during the pilot.

3. What you provide

  • A Windows 11 PC at home that stays on, with an administrator account for the install.
  • A new Gmail address you own, just for NookBird's mail, made before install day with our guide (about 15 minutes, including a forwarding rule from your own email). Your family's Telegram bot is made with you on install day, on your phone; it is yours.
  • Your intent to use it. Pilot places are limited, so by joining you say that you mean to use NookBird through the term as part of normal life and to tell us at the end what you thought. Along the way, honest feedback: a one‑line /feedback is enough; nothing formal.
  • Care with the secrets the setup gives you (the bot token, the mailbox password): they stay on your PC and are not shared.

4. Your family's information

  • It stays on your PC. NookBird keeps your mail, records and chat in its own folder on your computer. It does not send them to us. We cannot see them and we never ask for them.
  • Children. A child may have their own access to the bot; they see only their own things and never a parent's. Parents see everything.
  • You can always get it out or get rid of it. Export everything as a zip at any time; tell the bot to forget anything; clear all of it; uninstall. None of these needs our help or permission.
  • What leaves the PC: the text NookBird sends to the AI to read or write (section 5); a once‑a‑day check for updates that sends only the version number; the one activation request you email us (a code and a fingerprint of the PC, nothing about the family). Nothing else.

5. The AI during the pilot

NookBird uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to read mail and write briefs and answers. For the pilot it runs on an account we hold and pay for, so you need not set one up. That means the text it processes passes through our Anthropic account: we can see how much is used (the cost), never what was said. Anthropic's terms for business use apply (your content is not used to train their models). On 31 December the pilot key is turned off; if you keep NookBird, it moves to an account of your own in one step, with our help.

6. Feedback and what we may use

We will use your feedback, and the counts and health signals NookBird reports to us (versions, errors, how often things are used; never your family's content), to improve the product. If we ever want to quote something you told us, we ask first.

7. What we do not promise

This is a pilot of early software. It may miss something, get a date wrong, or stop for a while. Do not rely on it alone for anything that matters; the school's own mail is still the source of truth. We are not responsible for a missed deadline, a wrong reminder or a lost message during the pilot, and we will fix what you tell us about as fast as we can. Nothing in this agreement limits rights you have under Australian Consumer Law.

8. Leaving

You can stop at any time, for any reason, by telling us; we help you export and uninstall. We may end the pilot or your part in it with a week's notice, with the same help. The pilot ends on 31 December 2026; what happens after is a separate offer we will make before then, with no obligation.
