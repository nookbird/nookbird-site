Two ways to take part

A NookBird box, lent by us

A small, quiet computer about the size of a book. We bring it, plug it into your home router with a network cable, and set it up with you. It needs no screen and nothing else of yours. Boxes are limited. At the end of the pilot we collect it, after giving you your family's information to keep.

Your own Windows 11 computer

NookBird installs on a Windows 11 PC at home that can stay on. It runs in the background; a laptop that sleeps will miss things. We set it up with you, in person or on a video call.

Everything else is the same either way: the briefs, your family's private bot, the weekly fixes, and the help from us.

What NookBird does

  • A short brief for the parents, morning and evening. What's on, what needs doing and what's new, so nothing slips.
  • Reminders to the right person. A child's reminder goes to that child; a payment reminder goes to the parents.
  • Talk to it in plain words. Tell it something has changed or isn't yours, and it sorts it out.
  • Answers from the mail you forward. "When is camp?" or "how much is the incursion?": ask, and it answers from the email.
  • It gets to know your family. The schools, classes and clubs that matter to you, with nothing to set up.

You choose what NookBird sees

NookBird needs its own Gmail account, a new address made just for it. It never reads your personal inbox.

From your own email, you set up automatic forwarding to that address for the emails you want NookBird to know about and act on: the school's, the clubs', or any other sender you choose. Our step‑by‑step guide shows you how.

NookBird sees only what you forward, so you are always in control of what it can see. That is also the most we could ever see, and only if you choose to share to help us improve NookBird. Stop forwarding a sender at any time, and NookBird stops hearing from them.

Between NookBird (www.nookbird.com; "we") and your family ("you"), for the Term 4 2026 pilot. Plain words on purpose.

1. What the pilot is

NookBird is a family assistant that runs in your home, either on a NookBird box we lend you or on your own Windows 11 computer, and talks to your family through your own Telegram bot. It reads the school and club mail that arrives in a mailbox made for it, keeps the family calendar and reminders, and sends the parents a short brief morning and evening. You are testing an early version so that we can make it good before it goes on sale. It will have faults; finding them is the point.

2. What we provide

  • NookBird installed and set up with you, on the box we lend you or on your computer, and updated through the term with weekly fixes.
  • For a box: the box and its power lead, on loan for the pilot (section 5).

  • No charge of any kind during the pilot.

3. What you provide

  • For a box: a place for it at home, with power and a network cable to your router. For your own computer: a Windows 11 PC at home that stays on, with an administrator account for the install.
  • A new Gmail account you own, just for NookBird, made before install day with our guide (about 15 minutes), and automatic forwarding to it from your own email for the emails you choose. NookBird reads only what you forward. Your family's Telegram bot is made with you on install day, on your phone; it is yours.

4. Your family's information

  • It stays in your home. NookBird keeps your mail, records and chat in its own folder, on the box or on your computer. It does not send them to us unless you choose to share them (section 7).
  • Children. A child may have their own access to the bot; they see only their own things and never a parent's. Parents see everything.

Nothing else.

While it is on we may log in to fix a fault; we never copy your family's information off the box, and we tell you what we did.

6. The AI during the pilot

NookBird uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to read mail and write briefs and answers. For the pilot it runs on an account we hold and pay for, so you need not set one up. That means the text it processes passes through our Anthropic account: we can see how much is used (the cost), never what was said. Anthropic's terms for business use apply (your content is not used to train their models). On 31 December the pilot key is turned off; if you keep NookBird, it moves to an account of your own in one step, with our help.

Each week NookBird emails you and us a short report of counts only, never a name, a sender, a date in your family's life or any words of your mail. We use it, your ratings and your feedback to improve the product.

Sharing more is your choice. If you agree on the join form, or in writing later, we may look at how NookBird handled your family's mail, so we can find and fix what it gets wrong.
