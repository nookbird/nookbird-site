Support

Need a hand? Email us at support@nookbird.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you saw, and we'll help you sort it out.

What to tell us

  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • What you saw. If there's a message on the screen, copy it word for word.
  • Email us from the address you used to apply or buy, so we know it's you.

Please don't send us your family's messages or emails. We don't need them to help you.

Before you email

The answer may already be in our frequently asked questions or on what you need.
