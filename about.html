About NookBird

NookBird started at home.

We're a family with more than one child at school. Like most families, we were getting more school, kinder and club emails than we could keep up with: newsletters, permission slips, payments, changed times. What mattered was always in there somewhere, and it was easy to lose.

So we looked for help. Every option we found asked us to hand our family's emails, calendar and children's details to someone else's servers. We value our privacy, and we weren't comfortable with that.

So we made NookBird. It reads the emails for you, tells each person in the family what matters, and keeps your family's information at home, on your own computer.

NookBird is made in Australia, for Australian families. If you have a question or an idea, we'd love to hear it: hello@nookbird.com
